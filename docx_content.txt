03  AI PIPELINE  (Claude-Powered)
The AI pipeline is the core differentiator. Every intelligence feature ΓÇö sponsor matching, pitch generation, ad placement, and pricing ΓÇö runs through Claude (Anthropic). The API is called server-side from the FastAPI backend, ensuring API key security and allowing response caching via Redis.

SCREEN 2  ΓÇö  Sponsor Finder (/sponsors)
Purpose
Core AI feature. User inputs podcast niche + recent episodes ΓåÆ Claude analyzes and returns ranked sponsor matches with fit scores, contact info tier, and brand safety flags. Filter by category, budget, or match score.
Key UI Elements:
Search + Filter Bar: niche input, budget range slider, category multi-select (DTC, B2B SaaS, Health, Finance, etc.)
AI Match Cards: sponsor logo, match score (0ΓÇô100, color-coded), fit rationale (2 sentences from Claude), category tags, estimated CPM range
Quick Actions on each card: "Generate Pitch", "Save to Pipeline", "View Brand Guidelines"
Batch Select: select 3ΓÇô5 sponsors ΓåÆ "Generate all pitches at once" ΓåÆ Claude batch processes
Brand Safety Flag: red badge if Claude detects potential content mismatch
SCREEN 3  ΓÇö  Pitch Builder (/pitch)
Purpose
The money-maker screen. Claude generates personalized pitch emails, the user can edit inline, and send directly or export to their email client. Three-panel layout: sponsor brief | pitch draft | email preview.
Key UI Elements:
Panel 1 ΓÇö Sponsor Brief: auto-filled from Sponsor Finder; editable fields for custom notes, past relationship context, budget ask
Panel 2 ΓÇö AI Draft: Claude streams the pitch in real-time (typing animation). Tone selector: Professional / Casual / Bold. "Regenerate" and "Improve" buttons send follow-up Claude prompts.
Panel 3 ΓÇö Email Preview: live preview of how the email renders. Subject line A/B variants (Claude generates 3). One-click "Open in Gmail/Outlook."
Follow-up Sequence Builder: after main pitch, Claude generates a 3-email drip sequence with suggested send timing (Day 3, Day 7, Day 14)
Export: Save as PDF pitch deck, copy to clipboard, or push to CRM
SCREEN 4  ΓÇö  Ad Placement Analyzer (/placement)
Purpose
Upload or link an episode ΓåÆ Claude analyzes transcript and suggests optimal timestamps for pre-roll, mid-roll, and post-roll placements. Shows predicted listener retention at each point.
Key UI Elements:
Episode Selector: connect RSS/Spotify; pick from latest 10 episodes; or paste YouTube/audio URL
Timeline Visualizer: horizontal scrubber showing episode duration with color-coded placement zones (green = high retention, red = drop-off risk)
Placement Cards: for each recommended slot ΓÇö timestamp, predicted reach %, slot type, suggested ad length, revenue estimate
Engagement Heatmap: listener retention curve overlaid with ad placement markers (like YouTube Studio analytics)
One-Click Apply: send placement spec to the Pitch Builder with timestamps pre-filled for sponsor deliverables
SCREEN 5  ΓÇö  Pricing Intelligence (/pricing)
Purpose
Claude calculates fair market rates based on niche, audience size, engagement, and sponsor category. Outputs three pricing tiers with justification and a negotiation floor.
Key UI Elements:
Input Form: monthly downloads, audience demographics (auto-filled from profile), niche, engagement rate, sponsor category target
Pricing Output: 3-tier card layout ΓÇö Starter (small brands, 1 placement), Standard (mid-size, multi-episode), Premium (exclusive, series sponsor)
CPM Benchmark Chart: bar chart showing your CPM vs. niche average vs. top-percentile podcasts in same category
Negotiation Guide: Claude outputs a 3-bullet negotiation strategy ΓÇö your floor price, when to offer bundles, how to handle counter-offers
Rate Card Generator: export a branded PDF rate card (auto-designed) with your photo, podcast stats, and pricing tiers
