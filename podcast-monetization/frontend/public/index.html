FAQ

Questions we actually get asked.

How does the AI find sponsors for my podcast? ↓
We maintain a curated database of 20+ sponsor categories with CPM benchmarks. Claude analyzes your audience profile and episode content to score every sponsor in the database, then returns the top matches with fit rationale and estimated deal value.
Do I need a large audience to use PodMonetize? ↓
No. Our smallest matched sponsors work with podcasts as small as 5,000 listeners/episode. Niche audiences often command higher CPMs than large general audiences. The AI factors audience quality, not just size.
How personalized are the pitch emails? ↓
Very. Claude writes each pitch using your show name, listener count, audience data, and the specific reason that sponsor fits your audience. It sounds like you wrote it — not a template. Average pitch strength score is 8.7/10.
How is pricing calculated? ↓
We use a formula: CPM benchmark × audience quality multiplier × deal type modifier × exclusivity factor. This produces a floor, recommended rate, and ceiling. The model is transparent — you can see every variable in the Pricing Tool screen.
Is my transcript data stored? ↓
Transcripts are processed ephemerally — they're sent to Claude for analysis and not stored on our servers. We store your podcast profile and analysis results, but not raw transcript text.
